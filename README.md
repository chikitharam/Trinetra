# Trinetra
TRINETRA is an AI-powered community-centric cyber threat intelligence platform that identifies, analyzes, and simplifies emerging cyber threats and delivers personalized security awareness for students, senior citizens, small businesses, and regional communities.
# TRINETRA

## AI-Powered Community Cyber Threat Intelligence

TRINETRA is an AI-powered, community-centric cyber threat intelligence
platform designed to identify, analyze, simplify, and communicate emerging
cyber threats to the communities most likely to be affected.

Cyber threats such as phishing, scams, identity theft, fraudulent
investment schemes, fake job offers, malware, and social engineering
continue to evolve rapidly. However, threat information is often fragmented,
technical, and difficult for ordinary users to understand.

TRINETRA addresses this problem by transforming threat intelligence into
clear, understandable, and community-specific security awareness.

---

## Key Idea

Instead of providing the same cyber threat information to everyone,
TRINETRA personalizes threat intelligence according to the user's community.

Users can select a community such as:

- Students
- Senior Citizens
- Small Businesses
- Regional Communities

The platform then prioritizes and presents threats that are most relevant
to that community.

---

## Features

### Community-Specific Threat Intelligence

Threats are classified according to the communities they affect and
presented through personalized feeds.

### AI-Powered Threat Analysis

AI analyzes emerging cyber threat information and extracts important
information such as:

- Threat type
- Target community
- Attack method
- Severity
- Platform
- Region
- Indicators
- Recommended actions

### Threat Simplification

Complex cybersecurity information is converted into clear,
easy-to-understand language for ordinary users.

### Personalized Threat Feed

Users receive a prioritized feed based on their selected community,
threat severity, and relevance.

### Threat Matrix

Users can search and filter threats based on:

- Community
- Severity
- Platform
- Threat type

### AI Cybersecurity Assistant

TRINETRA includes a conversational AI assistant that can answer
cybersecurity questions, analyze suspicious situations, explain threats,
and provide practical security guidance.

The assistant also maintains conversational context for follow-up questions.

### Incident Reporting

Users can report suspected cyber incidents through the platform.

### Helpline Directory

Community-relevant cybersecurity support and reporting resources are
provided through a dedicated helpline section.

### Victim Experiences

Users can share cybersecurity incident experiences to improve
community awareness and help others recognize similar threats.

## Community Threat Coverage

### Students

Examples include:

- Fake internship scams
- Fake scholarship websites
- Phishing login pages
- Sextortion and blackmail
- Deepfake impersonation
- Exam-related scams
- Student loan scams
- Social media scams

### Senior Citizens

Examples include:

- Fake technical support
- Pension scams
- Investment fraud
- Banking and OTP fraud
- Romance scams
- Impersonation scams

### Small Businesses

Examples include:

- Invoice fraud
- Vendor impersonation
- Business email compromise
- Ransomware
- Payment gateway scams
- Fake supplier requests

### Regional Communities

Focus areas include:

- Region-specific scams
- Local-language threats
- Community-targeted phishing
- Festival and event-related scams
- Regional fraud campaigns
